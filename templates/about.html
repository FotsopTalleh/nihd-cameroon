About the Project

Understanding the mission, goals, and future of the National Internet Health Dashboard

The Problem

Cameroon faces significant internet connectivity challenges — from infrastructure gaps in northern regions to inconsistent ISP performance in urban centres. Until now, there has been no centralized, publicly accessible platform for monitoring national internet health.

Citizens, businesses, researchers, and regulators have lacked the data needed to make informed decisions about connectivity, investments, and policy.

Project Objectives
Real-time Monitoring
Continuously measure latency, uptime, and packet loss across all 10 regions of Cameroon.
Identify Bottlenecks
Detect and report performance degradation, routing anomalies, and ISP-level issues promptly.
Inform Stakeholders
Provide actionable data to citizens, businesses, policymakers, and regulators.
Promote Accountability
Hold ISPs accountable through transparent, independent performance benchmarking.
Target Users
Regulators & Policymakers

Use data to formulate evidence-based internet policy and licensing decisions.

ISPs & Telecoms

Benchmark performance and identify network improvement opportunities.

Researchers & Academia

Download raw datasets for academic research and network analysis studies.

General Public

Understand internet quality in their region and compare ISP offerings.
